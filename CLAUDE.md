# Shape of Stories — project rules

## THE READER SURFACE LAW (Tejas, 2026-08-01 — absolute)

People are not number-crunching machines. The charts exist so a person can LOOK and understand.

- The reading surface (charts, tooltips, labels, buttons, cards, ledes) carries ONLY what a
  normal person needs to see the story: the line, the shape's plain name, the events in words.
- NO numbers on the reading surface: no percentages, no scores, no r-values, no counts-of-corpus,
  no method names, no "(scaled to match)" parentheticals.
- Methodology, provenance, calibration, and every number live in the Method section ONLY —
  that section exists for the minority who go looking. One quiet link may point there.
- Every excerpt, label, and copy line must justify itself to a reader who only wants the chart.
  If it cannot, delete it. When in doubt, delete it.
- No inventory language ("114 stories · one line each" style dot-separated feature strings).
  Write sentences a person would say.

## Visual verification law

Every visual change is screenshot-verified before it ships, on EXTREME cases — steepest line,
densest annotations, both skins, light and dark — not one convenient example. Code review is not
taste review. New visual elements are SHOWN to Tejas before or immediately after shipping, as
images, not described in prose.

## Publish

The artifact redeploys from artifact.html (inline data). Never claim more than validation supports:
the validation numbers and their limits live in the Method appendix and in tmp/reviews/.
